What kind of food do you like? Do you like sweet, salty, sour, or spicy food? I like sweet food best. Let me teach you how to make something sweet and yummy.
First take a slice of bread. Cut it into two triangles.
Then put the egg, sugar and milk into a bowl and mix them together.
Put the bread into the egg mixture.
Then put some butter into a pan and heat it. Ask a grown-up, for example, your mum or dad, to help you.
Fry the bread in the pan.
Put the bread on a plate. Put some butter and jam on it.
Sit down and enjoy your French toast!
